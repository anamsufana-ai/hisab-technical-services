Package 1

Split AC

An indoor unit on the wall, an outdoor unit outside, connected by insulated copper pipes. We size every room so the units cool well without wasting power.

Best for: Apartments in new towers, villa bedrooms and majlis, small offices

What the package covers

  • Cooling load check per room
  • Supply of inverter split units
  • Copper piping, insulation and drain lines, routed before finishing
  • Outdoor unit mounting with brackets or stands
  • Pressure test, vacuum, gas charge, commissioning and handover

Good to know: piping routes are easier and neater when they are planned before plaster and paint.

Package 2

VRF / VRV AC system

Variable refrigerant flow (VRF, also sold as VRV) connects many indoor units to one outdoor system. Each room runs at its own temperature and the outdoor unit only works as hard as needed.

Best for: Towers, multi-storey buildings, large villas, schools, nurseries, heritage and public projects

What the package covers

  • Heat load calculation and system design
  • Indoor unit selection: ceiling cassette, ducted, wall-mounted or floor standing
  • Refrigerant piping, branch (refnet) joints and insulation
  • Individual room controllers and central control
  • Pressure testing, vacuum, refrigerant charging and commissioning
  • As-built handover

Good to know: VRF piping is planned at design stage. Bring us in early on a new build.

Package 3

Ducted split AC & ductwork

A concealed indoor unit sits above the false ceiling and sends cool air through ducts to several rooms. You only see neat grilles. We design, fabricate and install the ductwork ourselves.

Best for: Villas, open-plan living areas, showrooms and offices with false ceilings

What the package covers

  • Duct design and airflow calculation
  • Concealed ducted indoor unit and matching outdoor unit
  • Fabrication and installation of insulated ductwork
  • Supply diffusers, return grilles, filters, thermostat and controls
  • Air balancing, testing and commissioning

Good to know: good duct design matters as much as the unit. Wrong duct sizes make noise and leave rooms warm.
