Our story

Established in Dubai, 2023.

Hisab Technical Services LLC was established in August 2023 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and is registered with the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DED) under licence no. 1229557.

We design, supply and install split AC, VRF/VRV and ducted split air conditioning with ductwork for new buildings, towers and villas across Dubai and Sharjah, including heritage landmarks and multi-storey buildings.
